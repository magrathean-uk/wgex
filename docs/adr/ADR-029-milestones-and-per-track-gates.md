# ADR-029 Milestones and per-track gates

Status: Accepted 2026-10-25

**Decision.** Phase gates are split by track so the NE track never waits for the VM lab and no gate waits for hardware a contributor may not have. Milestones: **M1 macOS** (daemon, routes, DNS, pf, menu bar extra, onboarding, the cutover runbook with a dead-man rollback `wgex cutover --confirm-within 10m` and the previous client left installed), **M2 iOS/iPadOS** (engine, provider, domain, direct WireGuard on iOS, minimal UI, development signing), **M3 v1.0**. The cutover runbook and dead-man rollback are exercised in the VM lab before any real machine uses them.


## Drafts

**M1-03 draft - status truth and update negotiation.** Protocol version 5 remains exact-match and carries daemon-monotonic status sequence numbers, the active profile, wall and shared kernel continuous measurement times, operation-in-flight state and the existing recovery reason. Local launch/gap/invalidation snapshots have no measurement time. The composition reserves sequence ranges durably in the root-owned `status-sequence.json` journal beside the controller journal before publication, so restarts skip unused numbers without coupling status publication to controller generations; corruption prevents publication. An independent five-second dispatcher heartbeat reads controller bookkeeping without adding policy verification; clients run a separate fifteen-second reporting clock, showing Unknown with not-reporting copy while connected and last-known Stale after invalidation. Each connection has a serial bounded event pump; overflow of the 32-event queue delivers `subscriptionLost`, requiring resubscription and a status request. Callback deadlines are five seconds by default, ten for hello and thirty for start, stop and secure install; expiry invalidates the connection, with visibility-gated jittered reconnect backoff from one to thirty seconds. Compiled client/daemon compatibility floors are separate from the current build: a compatible newer app can prepare an older daemon for replacement, re-register through the guarded service controller and wait for enabled registration and a confirming hello, preserving honest update/approval copy and withholding secrets until builds agree; older clients are refused by newer daemons.


**M1-03 verifier addendum - accepted measurement semantics.** A heartbeat measures service responsiveness and does not renew protection evidence: `verifiedAt` and its continuous-clock counterpart record actual policy verification, and a client permits a fresh protection claim only for three heartbeat intervals after that verification. Recovery explanations are separate from consequence copy and reporter liveness. A sequence is allocated inside the atomic controller measurement; hello supplies a daemon-instance UUID so a new process resets the client sequence floor while retaining historical phase/events. A corrupt sequence journal self-heals from a time-derived floor, superseding the original draft's corruption refusal, and restart still reserves ranges before publication. Update preparation requires a user-initiated Finish update action, an administrator grant held through the prepare reply, and explicit registration/approval progress; reconnect never starts that administrative workflow. This paragraph records the verifier decisions.
